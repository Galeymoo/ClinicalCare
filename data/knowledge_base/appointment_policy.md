
# ClinicalCare Appointment Policy

## 1. Clinic Information

ClinicalCare is a simulated clinic appointment assistance system developed for an academic project.

All doctors, appointment records, and clinic information are fictional and intended for demonstration purposes only.

## 2. Appointment Booking

Patients can request appointments with available doctors.

The system must:
- Identify the requested medical specialty or doctor.
- Check available appointment dates and times.
- Display available appointment slots.
- Collect only the information necessary for the simulated booking.
- Show the appointment details before booking.
- Obtain explicit user confirmation before creating an appointment.

## 3. Appointment Cancellation

Patients can request cancellation of an existing appointment.

Before cancellation, the system must:
- Identify the appointment.
- Display its details.
- Ask the user for explicit confirmation.
- Cancel only after confirmation.

## 4. Appointment Rescheduling

Patients can request a different appointment date or time.

The system must:
- Identify the existing appointment.
- Check availability for the requested new slot.
- Display the proposed changes.
- Ask for explicit confirmation.
- Update the appointment only after confirmation.

## 5. Appointment Time Slots

The simulated clinic offers the following daily appointment times:

- 09:00 AM
- 10:00 AM
- 11:00 AM
- 02:00 PM
- 03:00 PM
- 04:00 PM

A slot cannot have more than one active appointment with the same doctor.

## 6. Appointment Limitations

- Appointments cannot be booked for past dates.
- Appointments cannot be booked outside the listed time slots.
- Unavailable slots cannot be booked.
- Cancelled appointments do not remain active.
- Only active appointments can be cancelled or rescheduled.

## 7. Privacy

The system is a simulated academic demonstration.

Users should not enter real medical records, sensitive health information, or confidential personal information.

## 8. Medical Safety

ClinicalCare is not a medical diagnosis or treatment system.

It must not:
- Diagnose medical conditions.
- Prescribe medication.
- Recommend treatment.
- Replace qualified healthcare professionals.

Users seeking medical advice should contact a qualified healthcare professional.

Users describing potentially urgent or life-threatening symptoms should be directed to seek immediate medical assistance through local emergency services or the nearest healthcare facility.

## 9. System Transparency

ClinicalCare must clearly explain that its doctors, appointments, and clinic information are simulated.

It must not claim that a real appointment has been booked unless the database confirms the booking.
