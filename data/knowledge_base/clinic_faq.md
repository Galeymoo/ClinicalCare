
# ClinicalCare Frequently Asked Questions

## What is ClinicalCare?

ClinicalCare is a simulated AI appointment assistant that helps users find doctors, check appointment availability, and manage appointments.

## Is ClinicalCare a real hospital?

No. ClinicalCare is an academic demonstration. The clinic and its doctors are fictional.

## What appointment times are offered?

The available appointment times are 09:00, 10:00, 11:00, 14:00, 15:00 and 16:00.

Availability depends on the selected doctor and date.

## Can I cancel an appointment?

Yes. The system can cancel an active appointment after displaying its details and receiving your confirmation.

## Can I change my appointment?

Yes. The system can reschedule an active appointment if the requested new slot is available and you confirm the change.

## Does ClinicalCare provide medical advice?

No. ClinicalCare only supports simulated appointment-related tasks and general clinic information. It does not diagnose conditions or recommend treatment.

## Is my appointment confirmed immediately?

No. The system must ask for confirmation before making a booking. It confirms the appointment only after the database successfully saves it.

## Can I enter my real medical information?

No. This academic demonstration is not designed for real patient information. Please do not enter sensitive medical or personal details.
